## Menu structures

| Format | Required structure | Catalog eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dinner | Existing 1–2 mains, 2 sides, optional dessert, 1 drink | Existing Dinner rules |
| Hotpot | Existing 1 safe shared broth, 2 proteins, 2 vegetables, 1 staple, 2 sauces, 1 drink | Existing Hotpot roles |
| Potluck | 2 mains, 2 sides, 1 dessert, 1 drink | Shareable dishes suitable for preparing and bringing as a whole |
| BBQ | 2 grilled mains including a plant-based main, 2 sides, 1 sauce, 1 drink | Grill-focused mains and suitable accompaniments |
| Picnic | 1–2 mains, 2 sides, 1 fruit/dessert, 1 drink | Portable food, with practical packing and storage notes |
| Brunch | 1–2 mains, 1 savory side, 1 fruit side, 1 drink | Breakfast/lunch dishes and distinct savory/fruit sides |
| Other | 1–2 mains, 1 side, 1 appetizer, 1 drink | Explicit general-gathering shared buffet |

Each new format must cover every guest with at least one safe substantial main and one safe side. All declared allergens exclude a dish for the whole group. Strict diets, intrinsic spice, separate optional spice, dislikes, adequate servings for the greater of expected and actual attendance, and the configured room budget keep their existing meanings. Recommendations remain deterministic and have materially varied food where the safe catalog permits it. No-solution reports identify the actual event, blocking rules, and affected guests; over-budget references cannot be voted on or finalized.

## Potluck preparation and costs

Contributions belong to finalized menu dishes. A contribution covers exactly the dish's planned servings and recipe; changing portions or ingredients requires the existing undo/reopen workflow. The owner commits to preparing or obtaining the complete dish, and the interface shows its scaled ingredients and estimated cost.

- A willing guest may claim an unowned dish, release their own dish, and change its readiness. The host may assign/release dishes to willing guests in this room and update readiness. Guests cannot take another person's dish or edit their readiness.
- Claim, release, and readiness actions are allowed only for the current finalized Potluck plan. A claim does not alter menu composition or votes. Room-serialized transactions make competing claims resolve to one owner, reject foreign IDs, and preserve state on failure.
- The room budget includes **all** food, including contributed dishes. Guest budget comfort remains advisory. The shared grocery estimate covers only unclaimed dishes; the contribution estimate covers claimed dishes. Their sum must equal the final menu estimate.
- Finalization generates shared groceries for all initially unclaimed dishes. Claims and releases atomically recalculate groceries for the remaining unclaimed dishes. Matching ingredients and units with unchanged or reduced quantities retain their rows, assignments, and purchase checks, including deliberate unassignments. Ingredients no longer needed are removed.
- Increased quantities retain their assignment but receive new unchecked rows. Old row IDs are rejected, preventing a stale purchase submission from checking off a larger quantity. New ingredients also start unchecked. When shared groceries already exist, claims and releases require confirmation explaining these changes.
- Transferring a dish between contributors leaves shopping unchanged. Readiness changes also leave shopping unchanged; changing the contributor resets only that dish's readiness. No-op ownership changes preserve all progress. When every dish is contributed, an empty shared list is a valid explained state.
- Undoing finalization clears contributions/readiness and shopping. Reopening preferences discards plans and dependent contribution data. Public sharing may expose aggregate contribution/readiness counts, never guest credentials, contact information, or private preferences.
