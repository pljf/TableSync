# Cooking plan

A finalized menu now has original TableSync preparation notes for all 66 built-in dishes, planned portions, equipment, and a suggested order: start longer work first, cook and assemble, then finish close to serving. This is an order suggestion, not a minute-by-minute schedule or an estimate of the whole meal's duration. Catalog times do not include every purchased grain's cooking time, chilling, transport, or extra batches.

The existing ingredient scaling remains authoritative: portions and units come from the selected plan, using the same scaling as shopping. Package counts are shopping estimates. Sauce bottles and jars are not a recipe ratio; seasoning instructions deliberately use small tasting amounts. Water for cooking is assumed, without adding other pantry ingredients or allergens.

## Content and confidence

The steps are authored for TableSync's ingredient sets and reviewed for ingredient consistency, practical preparation sequence and food-safety endpoints. They have not been kitchen tested. They are not copied from, or certified by, the linked recipe publishers. Manufacturer directions remain necessary for packaged products, dry grains and pulses.

Brownie mix is intentionally marked incomplete: required eggs, oil or other additions vary by brand and are absent from the catalog. The host must review shopping and food restrictions before following that package. Flourless lentil patties use a flat grill tray and can be served as a hash if they do not hold. The three-ingredient apple-oat preparation may be soft; the guide does not promise crisp, sliceable bars.

A guide is only applied when a persisted dish matches the original name, base servings, ingredient identities, quantities and units. Unknown or edited dishes get an explicit instruction to obtain their recipe, instead of a mismatched cooking method. Ingredient ordering and menu portion scaling do not invalidate a guide.

## Potluck and menu adjustments

Claimed Potluck dishes remain visible with their full planned quantities and contributor, but are excluded from the host's shared preparation order. Readiness is reported as saved; it does not assert a food-safety check. Spice-adjustment notes stay above the recipes. The guides use only the current ingredient sets and never import optional ingredients from an external recipe.

No schema or persisted menu changes are required. Public sharing continues to use the existing safe aggregate DTO and does not receive private contributor identities through this feature.
